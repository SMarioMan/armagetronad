Floor sampler level-of-detail diagnostic

The arena floor is textured projectively (GL_TEXTURE_GEN_S/T with GL_OBJECT_LINEAR), so the shader samples with texture2DProj. OpenGL derives the mip level of a projective sampler from the derivatives of the divided coordinate; several WebGL drivers derive it from the raw interpolated coordinate instead, which chooses a coarser level on a surface that runs away from the camera. That is the mechanism that can make distant floor grid lines disappear in the browser build while the desktop build keeps them.

The same grazing floor plane is drawn four times below. Whichever strip looks like the desktop build's distant floor is the computation the browser build should use. The numbers under the canvas are measured by this page from the rendered pixels, so they can be read out and reported without screenshot analysis: far grid contrast is the mean brightness above the local floor background in the distance band, lit px counts pixels that still show a grid line.
